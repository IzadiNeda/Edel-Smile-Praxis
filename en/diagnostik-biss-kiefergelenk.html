Functional Dentistry

Teeth don't function independently of one another

Bite, jaw joints, and chewing muscles form a complex interplay. In cases of grinding, clenching, recurring symptoms, or before more extensive treatment, we therefore look at the entire functional system — not just a single tooth.

Careful diagnostics help us better understand possible causes and plan treatment precisely.
